who we are

Two directors. One idea at a time.

lumora is a small, women-led studio. We keep the team tight so the people you meet on day one are the people who finish the work.

our story

We started lumora after years in big agencies, where good ideas often died in the handover.

So we built the studio around the opposite: two creative directors who stay on every project from the first conversation to launch day, a small circle of trusted specialists, and a simple rule, which is that nothing gets designed before it makes sense.

Today we work with founders, restaurants, startups and cultural spaces, creating identities and websites that are meant to stay in people's minds.
